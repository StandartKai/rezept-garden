# Glazed Pork Tenderloin

![Glazed pork medallions with cabbage salad|500](/img/user/images/glazed-pork-tenderloin.png)

## Ingredients
- 1 pork tenderloin
- Salt
- White pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 spring onions, chopped (whites and greens separated)
- Oil
- Sake
- Soy sauce
- Mirin
- Honey
- Sesame oil

### Cabbage Salad
- Cabbage, finely shredded
- Sesame oil
- Salt
- Rice vinegar
- Sesame seeds
- Cucumber, sliced

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prep the pork
1. Remove the silver skin from the tenderloin.
2. Slice into medallions.
3. Season both sides with salt and white pepper.

### Step 2: Sear and build the sauce
1. Heat oil in a hot pan, then sear the pork medallions.
2. Remove pork and add spring onion whites and garlic.
3. Saute on medium heat for 1-2 minutes.
4. Add sake and scrape up the fond.
5. Add soy sauce, mirin, honey, and sesame oil. Stir well.

### Step 3: Glaze the pork
1. Return pork to the pan on medium-low heat.
2. Glaze and reduce the sauce until sticky.
3. Finish with spring onion greens.

### Step 4: Cabbage salad
1. Toss shredded cabbage with sesame oil, salt, rice vinegar, and sesame seeds.
2. Slice cucumber and serve alongside.
