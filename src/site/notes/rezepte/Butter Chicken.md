# Butter Chicken

*Serves: 4*

## Ingredients
- 800g chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 can (400ml) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Basmati rice for serving

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Marinate
1. Mix yogurt, 1 tbsp garam masala, turmeric, and chili powder in a bowl.
2. Add chicken pieces, coat well, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight).

### Step 2: Cook the Chicken
1. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large pan over medium-high heat.
2. Sear the chicken pieces until golden on all sides, about 5 minutes. Set aside.

### Step 3: Make the Sauce
1. In the same pan, melt 2 tbsp butter. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute.
2. Pour in crushed tomatoes, remaining 1 tbsp garam masala, and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Stir in heavy cream and return the chicken to the pan.
4. Simmer on low for 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
5. Season with salt to taste.

### Step 4: Serve
1. Serve over basmati rice, garnished with fresh cilantro.

---

## Notes & Tips
- **Shortcut:** Skip the marination if short on time, but even 30 minutes makes a big difference.
- **Leftover Logic:** Keeps well in the fridge for 3 days. The sauce gets even better overnight.
