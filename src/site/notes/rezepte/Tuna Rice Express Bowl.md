# Tuna Rice Express Bowl

*Serves: 1 — 4 minutes, no cooking*

![Tuna, rice, edamame and cherry tomatoes in a bowl with soy and olive oil|500](/img/user/images/tuna-rice-express-bowl.png)

A no-knife, no-stove lunch built for muscle gain: ~800 kcal and ~52 g protein from one pouch, one can, and a handful of frozen beans.

## Ingredients
- 1 pouch microwave rice (basmati or wholegrain, ~250 g)
- 1 can tuna, drained (in spring water or olive oil)
- 1 cup frozen edamame (shelled soybeans)
- 1 handful cherry tomatoes, left whole
- Soy sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Heat
1. Tear open the rice pouch, tip it and the frozen edamame into a bowl.
2. Microwave 2 minutes, until the rice is hot and the edamame steamed through.

### Step 2: Assemble
1. Tip the drained tuna over the top.
2. Add the whole cherry tomatoes.
3. Finish with a splash of soy sauce and the olive oil.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- **Macros:** ~800 kcal, ~52 g protein. Scales up cleanly — a second can of tuna adds ~25 g protein for ~120 kcal.
- **Prep-ahead:** everything is shelf- or freezer-stable, so keep the components in a desk drawer and the freezer at work.
- **Swap:** salmon or mackerel in place of tuna; wholegrain rice for more fibre.
