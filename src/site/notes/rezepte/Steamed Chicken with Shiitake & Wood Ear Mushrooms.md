# Steamed Chicken with Shiitake & Wood Ear Mushrooms

*Serves: 2 · under 40 minutes (plus soaking)*

![Steamed chicken pieces with shiitake, wood ear mushrooms, ginger and spring onion on a shallow plate, with rice on the side|500](/img/user/images/steamed-chicken-with-mushrooms.png)

Cantonese home recipe from [@daigasikfaan](https://daigasikfaan.co/steamed-chicken-with-mushrooms): juicy chicken thighs and mushrooms in a savoury sauce, served with rice.

## Ingredients
- 3–4 boneless chicken thighs (about 450 g)
- 5 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 10–15 dried wood ear mushrooms
- 15 g ginger, thinly sliced
- 1 spring onion, chopped
- Rice, to serve

### Marinade
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- ½ tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- 2 tbsp mushroom soaking water
- 1 tsp sesame oil

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Soak the mushrooms (30 min)
1. Rinse the shiitake and wood ear mushrooms and soak them in hot water for at least 30 minutes, until soft.
2. Squeeze them dry, slice the shiitake and halve any large wood ear pieces.
3. Keep some soaking water for the marinade.

### Step 2: Cut the chicken
1. Cut the thighs into bite-sized pieces about 1 cm thick.
2. Leave the skin on if you like; its fat makes the sauce richer.

### Step 3: Marinate (15 min)
1. Mix the chicken well with all the marinade ingredients.
2. Fold in the mushrooms and ginger.
3. Marinate for at least 15 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.

### Step 4: Steam (12–15 min)
1. Spread everything in a single, even layer on a shallow heatproof dish.
2. Set a steaming rack in a deep wok and add about 5 cm of hot water.
3. Place the dish on the rack, cover, and heat on high.
4. Once the water boils, steam for 12–15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.

### Step 5: Serve
1. Scatter with the spring onion and serve hot with rice.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- **Shiitake:** Don't swap in fresh ones; you lose the deep umami.
- **Wood ear:** Optional (often sold as "black fungus"), but it adds bite.
- **Chicken breast** works too; steam it a little less.
- **Sugar** can be left out.
- **Dark soy sauce** is not tamari: it is thicker and milder and mainly adds colour. Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce and dark soy sauce are all at any Asian grocery.
- **Soaking water:** The original says 1 tbsp in the method and 2 tbsp in the ingredient list; just keep a bit extra.
