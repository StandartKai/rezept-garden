# Crispy Five-Spice Air Fryer Chicken Thighs

![Crispy five-spice chicken thighs sliced over white rice|500](/img/user/images/crispy-five-spice-air-fryer-chicken-thighs.png)

## Ingredients
- 4 skin-on chicken thighs
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- Five-spice powder
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- Rice, to serve

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Debone
1. Locate the bone and cut along both sides of it so the thigh butterflies open.
2. Slide the knife under the bone, cut it free, and remove it.

### Step 2: Marinate
1. Mix the thighs with soy sauce, a sprinkle of five-spice, sugar and garlic.
2. Marinate for 30 minutes; cook the rice in the meantime.

### Step 3: Air fry
1. Place the thighs in the air fryer basket, skin side up.
2. Air fry at 195°C (380°F) for 15 minutes, until the skin is crisp.
3. Slice and serve over the rice.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- The original recipe uses dark soy sauce and white pepper; regular soy sauce alone works fine.
