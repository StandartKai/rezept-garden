# Tomato Egg Stir Fry

## Ingredients
- 3–4 ripe tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 2 scallions
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- White pepper to taste
- MSG to taste
- Salt
- Sesame oil
- Cooking oil
- Splash of water

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prep
1. Slice the scallions.
2. Cut the tomatoes into chunks.
3. Whisk the eggs and season with salt.

### Step 2: Cook the eggs
1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat.
2. Scramble the eggs until just set but still slightly runny.
3. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook the tomatoes
1. In the same wok, sauté the scallions until fragrant.
2. Add the tomatoes with a little water.
3. Cook until the tomatoes soften and release their juices.

### Step 4: Combine
1. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, ketchup, white pepper, and MSG.
2. Stir until slightly thickened.
3. Add the scrambled eggs back in and mix to combine.
4. Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- Serve with a hot bowl of steamed rice.
