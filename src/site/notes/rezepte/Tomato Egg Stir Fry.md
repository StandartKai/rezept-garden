# Tomato Egg Stir Fry
![tomato-egg-stir-fry.png|500](/img/user/images/tomato-egg-stir-fry.png)
## Ingredients
- 3–4 ripe tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 2 scallions
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- Pinch of white pepper
- Pinch of MSG
- Sesame oil
- Neutral oil
- Salt

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prep
1. Slice the scallions. Cut the tomatoes into chunks.
2. Whisk the eggs and season with salt.

### Step 2: Scramble
1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat.
2. Scramble the eggs until just set but still slightly runny.
3. Set the eggs aside.

### Step 3: Cook the tomatoes
1. In the same wok, sauté the scallions until fragrant.
2. Add the tomatoes with a splash of water.
3. Cook until the tomatoes soften and release their juices.

### Step 4: Combine
1. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, ketchup, white pepper, and MSG. Stir.
2. Once slightly thickened, fold in the scrambled eggs.
3. Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- Serve over a hot bowl of rice.
