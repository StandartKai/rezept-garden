# Tomato Egg Stir Fry

![Tomato and egg stir fry in a wok|500](/img/user/images/tomato-egg-stir-fry.png)

## Ingredients
- 3-4 ripe tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 2-3 scallions
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- Pinch of white pepper
- Pinch of MSG
- Sesame oil
- Cooking oil
- Salt

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prep
1. Slice the scallions.
2. Cut the tomatoes into chunks.
3. Whisk the eggs and season with salt.

### Step 2: Scramble the eggs
1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat.
2. Add the eggs and scramble until just set but still slightly runny.
3. Remove the eggs and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook the tomatoes
1. In the same wok, sauté the scallions until fragrant.
2. Add the tomatoes with a splash of water.
3. Cook until the tomatoes soften and release their juices.

### Step 4: Combine
1. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, ketchup, white pepper, and MSG.
2. Stir until slightly thickened.
3. Add the scrambled eggs back in and mix to combine.
4. Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- Serve over a hot bowl of steamed rice.
