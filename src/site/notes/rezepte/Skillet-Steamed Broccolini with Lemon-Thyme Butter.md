*Serves: 2*
![broccolinis.png|500](/img/user/images/broccolinis.png)

## Ingredients
- 1 bunch Broccolini (washed and trimmed)
- 80 ml Water (increase to 120 ml if broccolini is very dry)
- Salt, to taste
- 30 g Butter
- Minced fresh Thyme
- 1 Shallot, minced
- Lemon zest (generous amount)
- Black pepper, to taste

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prep
1. **Size for even cooking:** Leave small stalks (<6 mm) whole. For medium stalks (6 - 12 mm), slit the bottom of the stalk in half while leaving the floret intact. For large stalks (>12 mm), cut the entire piece in half lengthwise.

### Step 2: Steam & Sizzle
1. Add 80 ml water to a 30 cm skillet and bring to a boil. 
2. Add the broccolini and a pinch of salt. Cover with a lid and reduce heat to a simmer.
3. After 3 minutes, remove the lid and toss the broccolini. Cover and cook for another 3–5 minutes until tender.
4. Remove the lid and let the remaining water evaporate until you hear a sizzling sound (the sign that the pan is dry).

### Step 3: Flavor Finish
1. Add the butter, minced thyme, minced shallot, lemon zest, and black pepper to the skillet.
2. Toss the broccolini in the residual heat until the butter melts and thoroughly coats the vegetables. Serve immediately.
