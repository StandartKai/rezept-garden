# Air Fryer Chicken & Potatoes

*Serves: 1 — 2 minutes active, 15 minutes in the air fryer*

![Air fryer chicken breast with potatoes, broccoli and a spoon of hummus|500](/img/user/images/air-fryer-chicken-and-potatoes.png)

Two minutes of hands-on work for ~850 kcal and ~65 g protein. A muscle-gain lunch that doesn't need a stove.

## Ingredients
- 250 g chicken breast
- ½ pack pre-cooked vacuum-packed potatoes
- Frozen broccoli florets
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Chicken seasoning or sweet paprika + salt
- 2 tbsp hummus

---

## Instructions
### Step 1: Season
1. Rub the chicken breast with the olive oil and the seasoning.
2. Halve the potatoes (or leave whole).

### Step 2: Air fry
1. Air fry chicken and potatoes together at 180–190°C for 14–16 minutes, until the chicken reads 72°C at the centre.

### Step 3: Broccoli and plate
1. Microwave the broccoli with 2 tbsp water, covered, for 3 minutes.
2. Plate everything with a thick spoon of hummus alongside.

---

## 💡 Notes & Tips
- **Macros:** ~850 kcal, ~65 g protein.
- **Batch it:** air fry 500 g chicken at once and keep half cold for the next day's bowl.
- **Doneness:** thick breasts may need a couple of extra minutes — check with a thermometer rather than the clock.
